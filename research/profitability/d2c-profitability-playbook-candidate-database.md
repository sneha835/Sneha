# D2C Profitability Playbook — Candidate Universe Database (Phase 1/3)

Project: "The D2C Profitability Playbook — India + Global"
Status: Phase 1 screening database. This is NOT deep research — every row reflects a live-search screening pass (see `sources/d2c-profitability-playbook-phase1-sources.md` for full citations). Deep dives (full Company Research Framework, primary-source triangulation) are reserved for the 18 companies selected in `d2c-profitability-playbook-phase1-summary.md`.

Evidence-type key used in the Profitability Status column: **A**=Verified fact (filed/audited), **B**=Company-reported claim, **C**=Third-party reporting (usually press citing RoC/Companies House/aggregator data), **D-bucket**=struggling/failed category (not to be confused with evidence label D). Where "Bucket" is used below it refers to the Phase-2 profitability category (A/B/C/D as defined in the methodology), not the evidence label — both are noted where they could conflict.

Total candidates: **76** (25 India, 25 US, 26 UK/Europe/Australia).
